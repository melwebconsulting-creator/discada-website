// THE COWBOY PLOWSHARE

NOT A GRILL. NOT A PAN.
THIS IS A DISCADA.

Originating in northern Mexico, Discada cooking utilizes a massive tractor plow disc seasoned like cast iron. Mounted over an open fire of Texas oak and mesquite wood, meats cook in their own concentrated juices in concentric thermal zones.

THE DISK

100-Lb Steel Plow Disc

Heavy steel disc retains massive thermal energy, creating a sizzling moat of flavor where meats fry, braise, and crisp simultaneously.

🥩

The 5-Meat Secret Blend

Top sirloin steak, pork tenderloin, thick-cut bacon, artisan chorizo, and smoked sausages, diced finely and layered in sequence over 8 hours.

  • ✔ Sirloin Steak & Pork Tenderloin braised tender
  • ✔ Crisp Bacon & Smokey Chorizo rendered fat
  • ✔ Diced roasted poblano peppers & caramelized onions
🪵

100% Texas Mesquite & Oak

No gas burners. No stoves. Authentic open fire imparts a deep, smoky undertone that permeates every layer of taco filling.

  • ✔ Seasoned Texas Post Oak & Honey Mesquite
  • ✔ Continuous live embers at 600°F central heat
  • ✔ Natural woodsmoke aroma infused in every bite
🌶️

Daily Fire-Roasted Salsas

Housemade salsa roja with charred habaneros & serranos, and salsa verde crushed fresh with tomatillos, garlic, and Mexican oregano.

  • ✔ Roasted daily over live disc coals
  • ✔ Zero preservatives, pure chili flavor
  • ✔ Custom spice level crafted for balance
🌽

Double Dipped Corn Tortillas

Local Austin masa tortillas dipped directly into the seasoned discada pan drippings and seared around the outer disc ring.

  • ✔ Double layer holds juicy discada filling
  • ✔ Crisp edges with savory, rich tortilla aroma
  • ✔ Topped with fresh cilantro, onions & lime

THE TACO MAFIA JOURNEY

2018

The Backyard Experiment

Two Austin natives restored a 100-lb farm tractor plow disc, built a mesquite fire pit in East Austin, and invited neighbors to sample cowboy discada tacos.

2020

The Vintage Truck Launch

Acquired and converted a classic vintage food truck, setting up permanent camp on East 6th Street. Word spread like wildfire across Texas foodies.

2023

Best Tacos in Austin

Voted "Best Food Truck in Austin" by Austin Chronicle readers and featured on national street food documentaries.
